The Long Return Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Summary

The developer does not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising service, tracking service, or gameplay server for The Long Return. The game does not send personal or gameplay data to the developer. Optional Game Center features are provided by Apple.

Data stored on your device

Match saves, tutorial progress, and settings such as sound, music, haptics, and the FPS probe are stored locally on your device. The developer cannot access this information. Deleting the app removes data managed by the app, subject to the operating system's normal behavior.

If a match score or achievement cannot be sent to Game Center, the pending score or achievement identifier is stored locally so the game can retry after a later successful sign-in. This pending data is not sent to the developer.

Optional Game Center

You can play against the game's AI without signing in. If you choose to use Game Center, Apple's GameKit service processes your Game Center account and player information, display name, submitted match scores, and achievement progress so Apple can provide sign-in, achievements, and leaderboards. Other Game Center players may see information according to Apple's service and your Game Center settings.

Optional 1v1 auto-match and friend invitations use Apple's Game Center service. Match orders and game state travel through Game Center between the two players; the developer operates no multiplayer server and receives no match data. The game includes no public chat or user-generated content.

The Long Return does not send this information to the developer. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and your Apple/Game Center settings.

Developer data collection and sharing

The developer does not receive, collect, sell, or share player data through the game. The Long Return contains no advertising identifiers, cross-device tracking, developer analytics SDKs, or third-party cookies. GameCenterKit communicates only through Apple's GameKit APIs and operates no independent service.

Children

The developer does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. No developer registration or personal information is required. A parent or guardian can manage a child's Game Center access through Apple's account and parental-control settings.

Changes

If a future version changes these practices, this page will be updated and the effective date will change.
